About Me

Connecting with others through her creative passions.

Jessica Nakayama is an Interactive Media Studies Major at North Central College. She aspires to connect with others through her passions of photography, graphic design, and creative writing. While she enjoys capturing moments on her DSLR camera, Jessica also creates digital illustrations in Affinity Designer 2 on her iPad. She is heavily influenced by her Japanese culture and nature, driving her to explore her roots and inspire others through mixed media works such as origami and folding fans. Using her skills of Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign, she creates posters, photo collages, and page layouts. Jessica joined the Design Agency to gain hands-on experience to further these artistic skills in real world situations. Her career goals involve working for a publishing company or magazine to combine and apply these passions. In her free time, Jessica loves watching a variety of movies such as musicals, action/adventure, romantic comedies, and animation.

About The Project

This website was created by Jessica Nakayama as a student project for ARTD 245: Web Design at North Central College in Naperville, IL, in 2026. All original graphics were created using Photoshop, Illustrator, or Procreate, and the site was hand-coded using HTML and CSS in Dreamweaver. Text content and design theory are adapted from Flux Academy, Tilda Education, and Dribbble articles.
