My Work

Examples of how my work reflects my creativity.

Jessica Nakayama Logo, Logo Project for Intro Visual Art and Culture at the College of DuPage, Created in Affinity Dsigner 2 App on iPad, Used symbols of a camera and Japanese folding fan to represent my interests of photography and my Japanese culture within my artwork. I focused on creating movement and contrasting colors within this logo.
Self-Portrait Photo Collage, Photoshop Collage: Personal Statement for Digital Art and Design at North Central College, Created in Photoshop, Combined 12 images (took seven of them) to create a symmetrical self-portrait to reflect my interests and struggles.
Lightning Flies Light-Up Shoes Advertisement Poster, Product Advertisement for Digital Art and Design at North Central College, Created in Illustrator, First came up with the logo and the product which then evolved into this poster to promote these made-up shoes. I took the background image and focused on creating contrast through the use of complimentary colors and the size of the text.
Bird Frame, Frame in Frame and Color Concepts Assignment for Fundamentals in Photography at the College of DuPage, Digital Photograph, Captured this bird framed within the branches that evokes cooler tones.
